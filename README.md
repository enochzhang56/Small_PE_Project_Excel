# Target Screen: Digital Infrastructure (Excel)

[link to excel](https://1drv.ms/x/c/e4ba8883d1c17b2b/IQBNnxBhVkB8Ta96KvRuiPtnASt-1M-1FZhvQPzpuonIMw0?e=1vhxre)

**All companies and numbers in this project are AI-generated for practice.**

A small Excel model that screens 12 fictional lower-middle-market digital infrastructure companies (fiber ISPs, fixed wireless, tower and fiber services) the way a small private equity team might.

## What it does
- Calculates EBITDA margin, EBIT, unlevered free cash flow (UFCF), implied enterprise value (EV), net debt, equity value, net debt / EBITDA, EV / (EBITDA - capex), UFCF yield, and capex as a % of revenue
- Screens each company PASS/FAIL on EBITDA range, recurring revenue, and leverage
- Ranks the companies that pass by UFCF yield and summarizes the top 3

## Key metrics
- **UFCF** = EBIT x (1 - tax) + D&A - capex - increase in working capital
- **EV** = EBITDA x asking multiple
- **Equity value** = EV - net debt
- **UFCF yield** = UFCF / EV (annual unlevered cash flow per dollar of enterprise value)
- **EV / (EBITDA - capex)** = price paid for cash earnings after reinvestment

## Results
8 of 12 companies pass the screen. The top 3 by UFCF yield are Redstone Telecom, Summit Tower Services, and Ridgeline Wireless.

## Limitations
AI-generated data, invented asking multiples, a single-year snapshot, a flat tax rate, and illustrative screening thresholds.

## Skills practiced
Excel formulas and cell references, IF/AND logic, XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, Pivot Tables/Charts, ranking, conditional formatting.
